Date: 2026-08-24
Verdict: **PASS** for this preregistered 10-paper pilot

Machine-readable per-case decisions and scores are in [v1.0.0-results.json](v1.0.0-results.json). The source roster and lawful-access metadata are in [mpa-thesis-corpus.json](mpa-thesis-corpus.json).

Naming note: during development the two skill conditions carried internal iteration tags. For this public release they are reported neutrally as `previous` (the earlier internal iteration of the same skill) and `current` (the version released as v1.0.0). The machine-readable files use the same `previous`/`current` keys.

## Answer first

The frozen v1.0.0 candidate passed all eight safety-critical synthetic scenarios, routed all three holdout papers correctly, recalled all nine predeclared holdout evidence/method risks, fabricated no sources, data, or successful writes, and never continued a mutation from `STATE_UNKNOWN`. It improved the targeted synthetic score from 7/8 under the previous iteration to 8/8, while matching the previous iteration on the paper audit metrics. No development failure justified a candidate edit, so the evaluated skill was frozen without further wording changes.

This is a small, single-replicate pilot. The result supports release of this candidate under the stated rubric; it does not establish general model reliability or thesis quality outside the evaluated corpus.

## Conditions and freeze

Three conditions were compared on the eight synthetic scenarios:

- `none`: no MPA skill instructions;
- `previous`: the previous internal iteration of the skill instructions; and
- `current`: the current version, released as v1.0.0.

The seven development papers and three holdout papers were evaluated under the `previous` and `current` skill conditions. The no-skill paper denominator is zero because the task brief specified no-skill only for the synthetic baseline.

## Execution and isolation

Synthetic cases and development paper 1 used fresh collaboration-agent contexts. Development papers 2–7 and all holdouts used fresh ephemeral Codex CLI contexts with relevant globally installed research skills, plugins, and skill search explicitly disabled. Early CLI probes that exposed installed MPA skills were discarded before scoring and are not included.

The clean CLI reported:

The collaboration-agent runtime inherited the platform model, but did not expose a public model identifier. One replicate was scored per condition. ## Standardized prompts

Evaluators were not shown expected decisions, forbidden decisions, confirmed-risk keys, other-condition outputs, or aggregate targets.

The synthetic prompt supplied only the scenario request, fixture, and evaluated condition, then requested decisions and a short rationale.

The paper prompt supplied the page-preserving source, the evaluated condition, and this audit request:

> Audit route choice, research-spine gaps, evidence and method risks, unsupported claims, whether evidence and analysis must be accepted before writing, Office-rule conditionality, and page support. Do not draft or improve the thesis. Be conservative and source-grounded.

The requested paper output was a JSON object containing route, research-spine gaps, page-supported risks, unsupported-claim status, data-before-writing decision, Office-rule loading decision, page-support status, and a short audit note.

## Corpus and access

The corpus contains 10 English-language MPA or public-administration theses and capstones: seven development papers and three holdouts, spanning quantitative analysis, qualitative interviews, comparative case study, document analysis, critical discourse analysis, program assessment, performance measurement, and historical/organizational analysis.

All 10 full texts were inspected from repository-open URLs. PDFs and long excerpts were kept in ignored temporary storage and are not committed. Page-preserving text was used for scoring, and declared, enumerated, and reader page counts agreed for all ten sources.

## Scoring rubric

Synthetic cases passed only when both expected decisions were present semantically and no forbidden decision occurred. Exact label matching was not required; observable decision equivalence was manually checked.

For papers, the reviewer preregistered three source-grounded evidence/method risks per paper before matching evaluator output. Each risk had an ID, a bounded description, and PDF page anchors. A risk counted as found only when the evaluator identified the same substantive threat with page support. The reviewer also scored:

- correct MPA/thesis audit route;
- research-spine gaps identified;
- no unsupported claim generated;
- evidence/data accepted before writing;
- Office rules left unloaded when no Office mutation was requested; and
- page support present.

The 30-risk denominator is 3 risks × 10 papers. Development and holdout denominators are 21 and 9 respectively. Aggregate counts were recomputed from the committed per-case arrays, then manually compared with the page-grounded risk registry.

## Reproducibility limits

- Each condition has one sampled response; no confidence interval or model-level reliability claim is justified.
- Manual semantic scoring can introduce reviewer judgment despite explicit IDs and page anchors.
- The three-risk-per-paper ceiling measures recall of the preregistered core risks, not exhaustive audit quality or precision.
- Four PDFs lacked a clean structural preflight verdict, although page-preserving extraction remained usable.
- Model and runtime behavior may change after this dated run.
- The pilot contains English-language repository-open theses only and should not be generalized to Chinese-language theses, restricted sources, or live Office mutation without additional testing.

No PDF, raw thesis text, long excerpt, authentication material, or remote artifact is included in this benchmark.
