# MPA Case Competition

Applies to China graduate public-management case competition entries (中国研究生公共管理案例大赛) and comparable case-writing tasks: planning the entry, structuring documents, organizing fieldwork evidence, selecting theory, and revising drafts.

## Rule precedence

The current-year official competition notice overrides this file. Before planning, verify the document set, word limits, formatting, and originality rules against that notice.

## Entry structure (tenth-edition baseline)

- One document containing 案例正文 (≤15000 characters), 案例分析报告 (≤15000 characters), and 调研报告 (≤4000 characters); each part ends with an accurate word-count mark, and appendices are placed after the case analysis report.
- 案例正文 must rest on real events and show truth, typicality, and conflict: a core decision-maker, key events, and an explicit point of dispute.
- Follow the 起承转合 narrative arc: 起 opens the scene with time, place, and actors; 承 introduces key events and disputes; 转 deepens the dilemmas and constraints; 合 drives the decision point with its opportunities and constraints.
- 案例分析报告: theory basis with an applicability justification, a constructed analytical framework, section-by-section analysis, innovative yet feasible and systematic recommendations, conclusion, and references.
- 调研报告: first-hand evidence base, event timeline, policy background, implementation challenges, and actionable or replicable recommendations.
- No entry part may reveal the team, supervisor, or institution identity.

## Fieldwork evidence

- Plan multi-stakeholder interview outlines (decision-makers, agency or street-level executors, enterprises or social organizations, residents) with interview dates; add questionnaires plus document and photo evidence where available.
- Triangulate statements across stakeholders before asserting facts; record who provided which information and when.

## Theory and framework practice

- Choose one to three public-management theories and justify applicability before analysis; award-level entries typically couple theories into one framework (for example, multiple-streams with holistic governance, strategic action fields with policy entrepreneurship, or environment-actor-action) rather than applying a single textbook model mechanically.
- Derive the analytical framework from the selected theory, apply it section by section, and close with mechanism findings plus layered recommendations.

## Originality and AIGC boundaries (hard constraints)

- Text similarity must stay within the notice's limit (≤10% on CNKI, citations excluded), AI-generated content must be red-marked and within the notice's share limit (≤10%), and the main framework, core arguments, key data analysis, innovative analysis, and conclusions must not be AI-generated.
- Therefore this controller assists with structure planning, evidence organization, theory comparison, framework critique, and revision feedback; it never substitutes for the team's own drafting of core original content. State this boundary before substantial competition-entry help.
