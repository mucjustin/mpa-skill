# MPA Case Competition

For China graduate public-management case competition entries (中国研究生公共管理案例大赛) and comparable case-writing tasks.

## Precedence

The current-year official notice overrides this file. Word limits, format, originality rules — check the notice first, every year.

## Structure

One document, three parts: 案例正文 (≤15000 characters), 案例分析报告 (≤15000 characters), 调研报告 (≤4000 characters) — notice limits, verify each year. Each part ends with its word count. Appendices follow the analysis report.

- 案例正文: a real event with a core decision-maker, key events, and an explicit point of dispute. Truth, typicality, conflict — no invented facts.
- Narrative arc 起承转合: 起 opens time, place, actors; 承 builds the events and disputes; 转 deepens the dilemma and constraints; 合 lands on the decision point.
- 案例分析报告: theory with applicability justification, one framework derived from it, section-by-section analysis, feasible recommendations, conclusion, references.
- 调研报告: first-hand evidence base, event timeline, policy background, implementation challenges, actionable recommendations.
- No part reveals the team, supervisor, or institution.

## Fieldwork

Plan multi-stakeholder interview outlines — decision-makers, street-level executors, organizations, residents — with dates. Add questionnaires, documents, photos where they exist. Cross-check claims across stakeholders before stating facts.

## Theory

One to three theories, each justified for applicability before use. Couple them into one framework and apply consistently. Stacked theories with no fit analysis are decoration.

## Originality and AIGC (hard limits)

- Similarity within the notice's limit (≤10% on CNKI, citations excluded).
- AI-generated content must be red-marked and within the notice's share limit (≤10%). Framework, core arguments, key data analysis, and conclusions must not be AI-generated.
- This controller plans, organizes evidence, critiques, and revises; it never substitutes for the team's own drafting of core original content.
